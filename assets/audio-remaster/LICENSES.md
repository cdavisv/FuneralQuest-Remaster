# "Original, remastered" audio ledger

What every file in this directory is, and what is not settled about it.

- **Status.** No audio has been generated for this pack yet. `manifest.json` lists no tracks, so a player who picks "Original, remastered" hears the Synth pack everywhere. `brief.json` and `sources.json` hold, for each of the 40 original 2001 sounds (35 distinct recordings), the local measurements, the description and the text prompt ready to generate, with a credit estimate.
- **What the files will be.** New audio generated with ElevenLabs (`eleven_music_v2_5` for music, `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` for ambience and effects) under the project owner's ElevenLabs account, from text prompts written for this project to resemble the 2001 game's sounds in kind, instrumentation, tempo, key, mood and length; then post-processed by `packages/audio` (loops cut and crossfaded, one-shots trimmed, mastered and encoded as the other packs are). Each file will be listed here with its model, generation id and date, and its prompt, parameters and cost in `sources.json`.
- **No original audio was used.** The 2001 sounds were analysed only on the owner's machine (`packages/audio/scripts/remasterDescribe.py`: durations, tempo, key, spectra, envelopes, onsets and a local audio tagger's labels). No original audio, and nothing derived from its samples other than those numbers and words, was uploaded to any service or given to a generator as input. The prompts describe style, instruments, tempo and mood, never the original's notes. These are not the 2001 recordings.
- **Licence: not established here.** Like the Studio pack, these files are not covered by the project's licence. Their redistribution terms are those of ElevenLabs' terms of service for the plan the owner's account is on at the time of generation, for the owner to confirm before the pack is made the default or shipped in any build. The Synth pack remains fully generated from this repository's own code.
- **Cost so far.** 0 credits.
