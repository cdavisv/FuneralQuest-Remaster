# The Expanded audit

Every player-facing feature of the Expanded ruleset (`docs/expanded-rules.md` sections 3 to 10, 16 to 24 and the eight newer places of sections 30 and 31, presented as sections 11 and 26 say), played through the web client against the real server by [`apps/client/e2e/expanded-audit.spec.ts`](../apps/client/e2e/expanded-audit.spec.ts): 38 tests in one serial run, in the `online` project, Chromium and Firefox; skipped in WebKit for the same reason as the Classic audit (selling is too slow there for the budget). The expected text is the Expanded pack's (`expandedPack` from `@fq/content`, the pack the server's Expanded towns load), and Classic's `CLASSIC_TEXT` where an Expanded screen keeps an original line, matched as patterns with the placeholders left open.

The spec makes its own Expanded towns through the admin API (`POST /admin/towns` with `ruleset: "expanded"`) and removes them again; the eight newer places are played in a town of their own ("Expanded Places") by two parlors, so their break-ins, smears and complaints touch nobody else and their days can be forced freely. What chance rarely gives is reached through the town's Game Settings (turns, odds, visits; script odds 1 for the Twins), its Expanded rule numbers (`expandedRules`: news and story arcs off in the main town and certain in the living-town test, tag transfers from the first day), the sysop's player editor (money, karma for the perk paths, tags, hit points, luck) and "force new day" for everything that needs midnights (bingo, inspections, the ordinance week, the election's filing, voting and count, the story arcs, and every once-a-day action of the newer places). The newer places' numbers (`town.placesA`, `town.placesB`) are not among the numbers a sysop may set or read for a town (see "Found outside the client" below) and were being retuned while the audit was written, so the spec writes none of them down: a price is read from its button's tag and checked against the cash chip, an amount in a line is matched as a pattern, and a chance outcome is reached with the luck, karma and rating the player editor sets and by repeating within a bound, over forced days when the action is once a day. "Done" means the screen showed what the spec says and the test asserts it. The three layouts (1024x745, 1280x720, 390x844) were checked on 45 Expanded screens each (46 when a body-preparation choice comes) (buttons inside the cabinet, no overlap, no clipped label, no sideways scroll, dialogs on screen, map places reachable; a button scrolled out of sight inside a scrolling list does not count) and the pictures looked at; the map is also checked for its twenty places (none over another, over the "Deathville Map" title or under the town strip, and each reachable by its spot or its row in the list of places).

Run it with `cd apps/client && FQ_E2E_BROWSERS=chromium pnpm exec playwright test e2e/expanded-audit.spec.ts`. Each run builds the client into a folder of its own. Do not add `FQ_E2E_SKIP_BUILD=1` unless `apps/client/dist-build` was built from the code under test: it reuses that folder as it is, and an old one fails the graveyard test with defects fixed long ago. Result on 2026-11-12 (the three `test.fixme` of the newer places now live tests): Chromium 38 passed, about 5 minutes; Firefox (`FQ_E2E_BROWSERS=firefox ... --project=online-firefox`) 38 passed, about 6 minutes. (2026-11-11, the eight newer places added: 35 passed and 3 `test.fixme`. 2026-11-07, before the newer places: Chromium 26 passed, the three engine and pack defects of that round fixed the same day.)

## Defects

Fixed in the client (`apps/client/src`):

1. **The graveyard's monuments panel said "Every monument is built." on the graveyard's own screen** for a parlor with no monument (`components/MonumentLadder.tsx`: the next tier was read only from the stonecutter's card, which only his screen has). It now falls back on the View's `monuments` record (11.5), so the panel names the next stone and its price everywhere.
2. **A place's note disappeared when a panel took the parchment** (`scenes/CabinetScene.tsx`): at the Expanded bank the 2001 bank note ("You are at the city bank...") was not on screen at all, and neither were the graveyard's fog (the Design Doc's line), City Hall's note, or the hospital's while an injury was listed. Original material must not be dropped in Expanded (CLAUDE.md), so the note now follows the screen's text in the text well when a panel holds the parchment (a tour page's note, its page count, is left out: the tour's parchment shows it).
3. **The luck tube was drawn empty beside every place panel** (`scenes/CabinetScene.tsx`): on a place's parchment the tube and its "Best"/"Worst" are art, and only the note drew the level, so the monuments, ballots, bank, bingo, chains and back-tables screens showed an empty black tube. The level is now drawn under those panels too (not on the desk's parchment or the phone, where the panel is its own sheet or the luck is in the strip).
4. **The black market's cards showed the original's colour codes**, for example "`5(+5 luck)`$" (`state/useGame.ts`, `expanded/buttons.ts`): a button's `hint` and `detail` are plain strings, and the engine's `rewardSummary` puts codes in them. The client now takes the codes out of every `hint` and `detail` it receives (Classic Views carry neither). The engine side is listed below.
5. **The newer places' player pickers showed colour codes in their prompts**, for example "`` `wSergeant Bixby: `$"Who broke in on you?" ``" (`expanded/buttons.ts`, `plainButtons`): an overlay's `prompt` is a plain string, and the police desk's, the Gazette's and the health department's prompts carry codes from the pack (`placesA.yaml:49-50, :132`, `placesB.yaml:69`). The client now takes the codes out of a picker's or a text box's prompt as it does for hints and details (unit test in `test/expanded.logic.test.ts`). The pack side is listed below.
6. **The town strip covered the Gazette and Cousin Sal's Pawn on the map** at 1024x745 and 1280x720 (`styles/expanded.css`): the strip lay over the top of the picture, where these two buildings stand, so a click on either hit the strip ("intercepts pointer events"). On the map the strip now rides on the frame's top edge in one row (long labels shortened, each chip's tip still whole); on a phone it sits above the map as before.
7. **On a phone the map's places covered each other** (`styles/app.css`, `scenes/MapScene.tsx`): every spot is grown to 44px for touch, and with the eight newer places (about 27px wide at 390px) ten pairs overlapped. On a map of more than ten places the phone keeps the spots at their drawn size; the list of places under the map, with its 44px rows, is the touch target there.
8. **"Run a Smear" ran out of its plaque at 1024x745** (`styles/expanded.css`): an 11-letter label is drawn at the full size, and on a narrow priced plaque (five at the Gazette) its two lines overflowed the bottom. A short label on a priced plaque narrower than 175px now takes the long labels' size.

## What could not be driven, and why

- Which special customer comes, a sale exactly at the limit (PERFECT and its front-page item), a Mob Boss's threat, a pre-need future payment: chance, with no setting that forces them (`customer.*` and `scoring.*` are not tunable per town, by design). The spec accepts whichever special comes and whichever band the sale lands in, and asserts the line for it.
- The security upgrades turning a prowler away (15% a level) and the hit-man rumour (luck 60 or more): chance; not forced.
- A landed hit and the snitch: the target in the run took the Peace Pledge before midnight, which foils the hit (the test asserts the foiled outcome: the hirer's letter, or the HIT MAN SINGS page when the hit man talks).
- A loan collected or defaulted at its due day, chain dues a member cannot pay, and the season's last days (no loans in the last three, the final day's rules): they need the loan's ten days or the season's end; the soak run (`docs/ALPHA.md` A4) covers them through the engine and server.
- The newer places' remaining branches: a report or settlement against a rival with an empty till (`reportedBroke`, `settledBroke`), a fight won by an attacker as the attack reported (only break-ins were), the smear's refusals for a Peaceful or chain-mate target and the complaint's "no rivals" (the pickers never list those parlors, so the refusals cannot be reached from the client), a pawn lot refused for a full storeroom, the Grave Robber's higher price for loot, a lot or a truce's lead when the parlor already holds the most leads, and the expiry of unused oven slots and bouquets after their two days and of a truce after its days. None needs a chance the run cannot reach; they were left out to keep the run short, and `packages/core/test/placesA.test.ts` and `placesB.test.ts` cover them in the engine.
- The weather's effect on the florist's price: the spec checks the florist's line for the day's weather and that the price on the tag is what is paid, but not the multiplier, which it does not read.
