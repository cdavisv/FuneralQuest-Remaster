# The Expanded audit

Every player-facing feature of the Expanded ruleset (`docs/expanded-rules.md` sections 3 to 10 and 16 to 24, presented as sections 11 and 26 say), played through the web client against the real server by [`apps/client/e2e/expanded-audit.spec.ts`](../apps/client/e2e/expanded-audit.spec.ts): 26 tests in one serial run (23 that pass and 3 `test.fixme` that show engine or pack defects), in the `online` project, Chromium and Firefox; skipped in WebKit for the same reason as the Classic audit (selling is too slow there for the budget). The expected text is the Expanded pack's (`expandedPack` from `@fq/content`, the pack the server's Expanded towns load), and Classic's `CLASSIC_TEXT` where an Expanded screen keeps an original line, matched as patterns with the placeholders left open.

The spec makes its own Expanded towns through the admin API (`POST /admin/towns` with `ruleset: "expanded"`) and removes them again. What chance rarely gives is reached through the town's Game Settings (turns, odds, visits; script odds 1 for the Twins), its Expanded rule numbers (`expandedRules`: news and story arcs off in the main town and certain in the living-town test, tag transfers from the first day), the sysop's player editor (money, karma for the perk paths, tags, hit points, luck) and "force new day" for everything that needs midnights (bingo, inspections, the ordinance week, the election's filing, voting and count, the story arcs). "Done" means the screen showed what the spec says and the test asserts it. The three layouts (1024x745, 1280x720, 390x844) were checked on 34 Expanded screens each (35 when a body-preparation choice comes) (buttons inside the cabinet, no overlap, no clipped label, no sideways scroll, dialogs on screen, map places reachable; a button scrolled out of sight inside a scrolling list does not count) and the pictures looked at.

Run it with `cd apps/client && FQ_E2E_BROWSERS=chromium pnpm exec playwright test e2e/expanded-audit.spec.ts` (add `FQ_E2E_SKIP_BUILD=1` to reuse a build). Result on 2026-11-07 in Chromium: 26 passed (the three engine and pack defects below fixed the same day), about 3 minutes. Not run in Firefox for this report.

## Defects

Fixed in the client (`apps/client/src`):

1. **The graveyard's monuments panel said "Every monument is built." on the graveyard's own screen** for a parlor with no monument (`components/MonumentLadder.tsx`: the next tier was read only from the stonecutter's card, which only his screen has). It now falls back on the View's `monuments` record (11.5), so the panel names the next stone and its price everywhere.
2. **A place's note disappeared when a panel took the parchment** (`scenes/CabinetScene.tsx`): at the Expanded bank the 2001 bank note ("You are at the city bank...") was not on screen at all, and neither were the graveyard's fog (the Design Doc's line), City Hall's note, or the hospital's while an injury was listed. Original material must not be dropped in Expanded (CLAUDE.md), so the note now follows the screen's text in the text well when a panel holds the parchment (a tour page's note, its page count, is left out: the tour's parchment shows it).
3. **The luck tube was drawn empty beside every place panel** (`scenes/CabinetScene.tsx`): on a place's parchment the tube and its "Best"/"Worst" are art, and only the note drew the level, so the monuments, ballots, bank, bingo, chains and back-tables screens showed an empty black tube. The level is now drawn under those panels too (not on the desk's parchment or the phone, where the panel is its own sheet or the luck is in the strip).
4. **The black market's cards showed the original's colour codes**, for example "`5(+5 luck)`$" (`state/useGame.ts`, `expanded/buttons.ts`): a button's `hint` and `detail` are plain strings, and the engine's `rewardSummary` puts codes in them. The client now takes the codes out of every `hint` and `detail` it receives (Classic Views carry neither). The engine side is listed below.

Found outside the client and fixed in the engine and the pack the same day (each had a `test.fixme` in the spec, now a live test):

1. `packages/core/src/expanded/town/club.ts:338-341` and `packages/core/src/expanded/players.ts:146`: the market's `Button.hint` is `rewardSummary(...)`, which wraps the summary in colour codes (`` `5(...)`$ ``). Hints are plain strings. Fixed: `rewardSummary` returns plain text and the two result screens that show it colour it themselves.
2. `packages/core/src/expanded/town/bank.ts:137`: the teller's savings line rounds the rate to one decimal (`Math.round(rate * 1000) / 10`), so a 0.15% rate reads "0.2% a day" while the bank's books (`View.bank.savingsRate`) say 0.15%. Fixed: two decimals. Test "the teller's savings rate agrees with the bank's books".
3. `packages/core/src/classic/statLines.ts:29`, reused by `packages/core/src/expanded/modules/stats.ts:26`: "Visits left" on the stats sheet is `visitsPerDay - playerFights` and leaves out the cutthroat parlor's bonus visit (`bonusVisits`, `expanded/pvp.ts:68`), so the sheet says 8 where the row of parlors (`classic/modules/visit.ts:86-89`) says 9. Fixed in `expanded/modules/stats.ts` (Classic's `statLines.ts` is untouched). Test "the stats sheet counts a cutthroat parlor's extra visit".
4. `packages/content/packs/expanded/expanded/town/text/cityHall.yaml:86` (`votesDetail: '{votes} votes'`, filled at `packages/core/src/expanded/town/cityHall.ts:416` and `:578`): a ballot card with one vote reads "1 votes". Fixed: a `votesDetailOne` line in the pack, the starter and the schema. Test "a ballot option with one vote reads '1 vote'".
5. Minor, no test: the Expanded pack's `stats.labels.money` ("Cash") and `stats.labels.banked` ("Turns banked") are never used; the stats sheet keeps Classic's "Money" line (`expanded/modules/stats.ts` builds only its own extra lines), while the ledger calls the same number "Cash".

## What could not be driven, and why

- Which special customer comes, a sale exactly at the limit (PERFECT and its front-page item), a Mob Boss's threat, a pre-need future payment: chance, with no setting that forces them (`customer.*` and `scoring.*` are not tunable per town, by design). The spec accepts whichever special comes and whichever band the sale lands in, and asserts the line for it.
- The security upgrades turning a prowler away (15% a level) and the hit-man rumour (luck 60 or more): chance; not forced.
- A landed hit and the snitch: the target in the run took the Peace Pledge before midnight, which foils the hit (the test asserts the foiled outcome: the hirer's letter, or the HIT MAN SINGS page when the hit man talks).
- A loan collected or defaulted at its due day, chain dues a member cannot pay, and the season's last days (no loans in the last three, the final day's rules): they need the loan's ten days or the season's end; the soak run (`docs/ALPHA.md` A4) covers them through the engine and server.
- Firefox: the spec runs there in the full e2e suite but was run only in Chromium for this report.
